# Design and source research

These are dated studies that informed Paddock. The app's current features, requirements, and data sources are described in the [project README](../README.md).

The September 7 [Linear and Apple study](linear-apple/report-source.md) explored the original live-centered vision. Its [PDF](../output/pdf/linear-apple-design-research.pdf), [source ledger](linear-apple/claim-source-ledger.json), and [research log](linear-apple/research-log.json) preserve that study. Live coverage and iPhone proposals in it are historical recommendations. Paddock now focuses on native macOS replays.

The October 1 [free-coverage feasibility record](free-coverage/feasibility.json) predates the implemented app. Its probes and next steps describe that investigation, not current provider support. The October 2 [QA record](../output/qa/validation.json) also describes an earlier build. See the [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for data terms and the [publication assessment](../output/audit/publication.md) for remaining rights questions.
