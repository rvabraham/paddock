## Historical timing archives and marks

For 2018–2022 sessions and later qualifying sessions, Paddock's native historical reader can request completed-session files under `livetiming.formula1.com/static/`. Paddock does not connect to the direct live timing service. Historical HTTP acceptance does not grant rights to redistribute archive data. Formula 1's [public guidelines](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/guidelines.4EOKE9RRqevL4niTK9kWyt) and [legal notices](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/legal-notices.7egvZU48hzrypubGBNcQKt) assert rights over timing data and restrict reproduction and app use. These historical files are not covered by Paddock's MIT license or OpenF1/Jolpica's data notices above. Their use remains subject to applicable law and service terms; no upstream clearance is represented here.

[FastF1](https://github.com/theOehrly/Fast-F1) source was consulted to understand historical archive formats. Its [MIT license](https://github.com/theOehrly/Fast-F1/blob/master/LICENSE) covers its software, not upstream timing data or service access. Paddock does not bundle FastF1, Python, or its dependencies. Downloads are stored on the user's Mac; Paddock does not operate a race-data proxy or mirror.

Paddock is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, the teams, or the data providers. Formula 1 and related marks belong to their owners. The icon is generated by Paddock's own drawing script; no Formula 1 logo, official typeface, driver headshot image, team logo, race video, or team radio recording is bundled. Provider JSON can contain links to third-party images or pages. Those links do not license the linked material.
